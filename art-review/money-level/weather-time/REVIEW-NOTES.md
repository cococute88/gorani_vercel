# Phase A visual review notes

Reviewed on the standalone gallery with the approved base shown in Variant, Original, 50/50 Overlay, and Flicker modes.

## Morning

- Sunny: clear dawn-to-morning transition, cool violet residual light, low warm sun, mist, and pale warm pond reflection; readable rather than night-dark.
- Cloudy: retains the dawn horizon and morning color split under soft gray-blue clouds; dry and visibly calmer than Rain.
- Rain: wet path and dock, rain haze, fine rain, cool vegetation, and dawn reflection remain legible.
- Storm: clearly stronger than Rain through layered dark cloud, lightning, heavy haze, and storm pond; the warm dawn breach preserves the row identity.

## Day

- Sunny: brightest and most neutral state; clean greens, blue pond, soft high sun, and comfortable baseline contrast.
- Cloudy: diffused daylight and reduced distant contrast without rain, wetness, or storm darkness.
- Rain: readable daytime rain with damp terrain, cool pond, and natural fine rainfall.
- Storm: independent storm lighting, stacked clouds, lightning, wet terrain, and agitated pond while terrain remains readable.

## Evening

- Sunny: unmistakable golden hour with orange/peach sky, warm mountains/path, and strong gold pond reflection.
- Cloudy: sunset survives under soft mauve-gray cloud; no rain cues.
- Rain: cool rain and wet surfaces coexist with a visible orange sunset band and warm reflections.
- Storm: strongest dramatic warm/cool contrast, lightning and storm cloud, with sunset light retained at the horizon and across wet ground/water.

## Night

- Sunny: clear stars/moon, blue-teal forest, and strong but controlled moon reflection; detail remains readable.
- Cloudy: soft moon behind thick cloud, lowered contrast, no rain or lightning.
- Rain: wet night scene with fine rain, rain haze, damp terrain, and pond ripples while keeping landmarks visible.
- Storm: lightning-defined terrain, heavy rain, layered violet/navy cloud, and turbulent steel-blue pond; distinct from ordinary night rain.

## Geometry and content checks

- Required canvas: 1672×941. All 16 accepted masters pass.
- Checked in 50/50 Overlay and Flicker modes: pond boundary, shoreline, dock, central path, side clearings, fence runs, rocks, major tree silhouettes, bench, stump, garden bed, and mountain/horizon placement.
- No house, camp, character, label, fishing rod, HUD, button, or other prohibited runtime object was baked into the accepted backgrounds.
- Reject/retry count: 1. The first Evening Rain result was 1671×941 and was replaced by a regenerated 1672×941 master.

## Remaining visual concerns for approval

- Lightning visibility in Storm assets is intentionally pronounced; final intensity is an art-direction choice.
- Evening wet-state reflections are vivid by design and should be compared at intended runtime display size before Phase B compression.
- Night Sunny uses a strong moon path on the pond; confirm that this does not compete with future foreground characters.
